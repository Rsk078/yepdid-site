The task page

Done means both of you say so.

Every shared task takes two ticks. The one doing it taps Mark done. The other sees Confirm? and makes it official, and then comes the confetti.

Not quite done? Tap Not yet and send it back with a reason. Waiting on them? The little hand pokes your partner, and the bell sets a reminder for you.

Home

Your shared board, already sorted.

Home sorts everything into Waiting for you, Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This week and Later. The ring around your face fills as today's tasks get ticked.

Tap a day on the strip to see just that day. Filter by All, Mine, your partner's name or Private. Swipe a task right to mark it done, or tap it to open it.

New task

A new task in a few taps.

Write it, choose who it's for (Me, your partner or Either) and when. Tap a day and a time and it's set, with the full date right on the Due chip. Quick picks cover Today, Tomorrow and This weekend.

Busy time is off limits: try to put a deadline there and it shakes its head. Add a checklist, attach a photo or PDF, or make the whole task Private so only you see it.

Money

Split it. Yepdid keeps the score.

Add what one of you paid and how it splits: Half each, all for the other person, or a Custom share. The card up top says who owes whom, and Settle up records a payback until you're All square.

It's a shared tally in rupees, not a wallet: no money ever moves through Yepdid. A finished task can carry its cost too.

Activity

Everything that happened, in one feed.

The bell keeps a running story of your board: who created, ticked, confirmed or said not yet, who added a cost, and when you paired up.

Need a nudge? A poke lands as a notification on their phone, like “👉 Ben poked you”. One poke per task a minute, so nobody gets spammed.
