Privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Yepdid is made by Rajeev Kushwaha. Questions: hello@yepdid.com.

The short version

  • We keep what Yepdid needs to work for you and your partner: your account, and the tasks (with any photos and PDFs on them), costs and busy times you add.
  • No ads, no advertising or analytics code in the app, and we never sell your data.
  • Private tasks are visible only to you. Your partner sees what you share with them.
  • Delete your account from the app at any time, and your data is deleted right away.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address, to sign you in with a one-time code. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's email and name from Google.
  • Profile: the name you choose, an optional profile photo you pick from your photo library, your colour, your time zone (so deadlines show at the right time), and your personal pairing code.

What you add

  • Tasks: titles, notes, checklists, deadlines, who a task is for, whether it's private, and who ticked or confirmed it and when.
  • Photos and PDFs you attach to a task, from your photo library, your camera, your files, or another app's share button. Photos are resized and saved as JPEG before upload. They're stored privately: only people who can see the task can open them, through links that expire after an hour.
  • Text or links you share to Yepdid from another app. They become a new task's title, notes or checklist, and are kept like anything else you type into a task.
  • Costs you record in Money: what it was for, the amount, who paid and how it splits. Yepdid only keeps a tally between the two of you. It never handles payments, cards or bank details.
  • Busy times you add.
  • Activity: a history of what happened on your board, such as who created, ticked or confirmed a task.

Pairing and invites

  • Pair requests between two accounts, and attempts to enter a code (to stop people guessing codes).
  • If you invite someone by email: their email address, your name, and a 6-digit code that expires after 48 hours. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version of the code.

Your device

  • If you allow notifications, a push token and whether your phone runs iOS or Android, so your partner's pokes, new tasks and cost updates reach you.
  • Reminders you set are scheduled on your phone only. They are not sent to us.
  • Your appearance and colour choices, and some cached data so the app opens quickly, are kept on your phone. Signing out clears the cached data.

What we don't collect

No location (only your time zone), no contacts or address book, no microphone, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other apps or websites. The camera opens only when you choose to take a photo for a task, and only that photo is kept. Yepdid has no ads.

Crash reports. Yepdid doesn't collect crash reports today. If we turn them on, we'll use Sentry, set up so reports don't include your name, email or IP address, and we'll update this page first.

How we use it

  • To run Yepdid: sign you in, sync your board between you and your partner, and keep it up to date in real time.
  • To keep deadlines out of busy time, and to show both of you on Schedule.
  • To send the emails you ask for: sign-in codes and partner invites.
  • To send push notifications when your partner adds a task you can both see, pokes you, or adds or settles a cost. Private tasks never send one.
  • To keep Yepdid secure and stop abuse, for example by limiting how often a pairing code can be tried and how many invites one account can send.

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles about you.

What your partner sees

  • When you're paired, your partner sees your name, photo and colour, the tasks you share, their checklists, photos, PDFs and timeline, your busy times on Schedule, the costs you record, and the shared activity feed.
  • Private tasks, with their checklists, files and history, are visible only to you.
  • If you unpair, you both lose access to the shared board.

Your profile photo is stored so the app can show it to your partner. Someone who had the photo's exact web address could open it, so choose a photo you're comfortable with.

Keeping it safe

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted.
  • Database rules mean only you and your partner can read your shared space, and only you can read your private tasks.

Deleting your account

In the app, open You, then Account, and tap Delete account. Deletion happens straight away and can't be undone:

  • Your sign-in account and email are deleted, along with your Google sign-in link if you used one. Google may still list Yepdid among your connected apps; you can remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Your profile photo and push tokens are deleted, and any reminders on your phone are cancelled.
  • Your boards are deleted: tasks, checklists, photos and PDFs, checks, costs, busy times and activity, including the shared board with your partner. If you're paired, your partner loses the shared board too.
  • We keep a nameless placeholder for your profile (no name, photo, colour or email), which can't be linked back to you.

Can't get into the app? Email hello@yepdid.com from the address you sign in with, and we'll delete your account for you within 30 days.

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. We don't keep backups of deleted accounts beyond our hosting provider's routine backups, which expire on their own.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your name, photo and colour on the You page at any time.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone's settings, or make any task private.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to hello@yepdid.com. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act give you these rights, and you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Yepdid isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Yepdid uses your data, we'll update this page and its date. If the change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
